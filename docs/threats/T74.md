# T74

## Threat

**Profile pictures, About you and personal instructions** (ADOPTION_PLAN X.29, README "Profile and Personalisation"): a person uploads an avatar and a banner, writes a line about themselves and standing instructions, and picks reply choices; the pictures are served to others, the text reaches the model in every chat of theirs. (1) Hostile pictures: an SVG or HTML named `.png`, a polyglot, a GIF, a header claiming 100,000 px a side, a truncated file, a 50 MB body, a slow body. (2) A picture fetched by someone who should not see it: a user sharing no workspace, an admin, the API token, a member removed from the workspace, nobody signed in; its existence confirmed. (3) Stored XSS through the name, About you or the instructions wherever they show (the account menu, members lists, Profile). (4) The instructions steering the model as the harness's text: a forged `[harness]` line, a line closing the section's frame early, control characters or line separators splitting a line. (5) Another person's Personalisation reaching a chat that is not theirs; an untrusted (webhook) turn reading it; an agent meant for a strict format getting it. (6) A section large enough to fail its own `context_sent` append (T52), or one that rewrites the prompt cache every turn. (7) Two tabs saving at once losing a part; the account deleted and its pictures or text left behind.

## Mitigation

(1) **Bytes, not names:** `harness_core::image::raster` decides the format from the first bytes, PNG, JPEG or WebP only (never SVG, never GIF), and `still` walks the whole structure without decoding a pixel (T17; every length checked, nothing allocated from a declared size, every step forward, fuzz-tested): it refuses **metadata** (a JPEG APP1, EXIF or XMP, or APP13, IPTC; a PNG `eXIf`, `tEXt`, `iTXt` or `zTXt`; a WebP `EXIF` or `XMP ` chunk or VP8X flag: an upload straight through the API is not re-encoded by the browser, and the review of PR 136 served a JPEG's GPS position to a co-member byte for byte), **animation** (`acTL`/`fcTL`/`fdAT`, `ANIM`/`ANMF` or the VP8X flag), a **second size** (more than one JPEG frame header; a WebP's VP8X canvas and its bitstream both count, the larger is bounded), and bytes after the end or a cut file; the size bounds: avatar at most 256 × 256 and 256 KiB, banner 1500 × 500 and 1 MiB (`http/profile.rs`, `check_image`); the body limit is the cap plus one byte (`DefaultBodyLimit`), the 30 s route timeout bounds a slow one.
The browser crops and re-encodes first (`profile/pictures.ts`), which strips camera data; nothing relies on it.
Stored in the blob store as the account's (`profile_images`, encrypted, counted against its quota, a new one replaces the old in one transaction and the old blob goes when nothing else holds it).
Served by `blobs::serve` with its rules: the stored type only to `Sec-Fetch-Dest: image`, `nosniff`, the sandboxing CSP, `Content-Disposition: inline`, `Cache-Control: no-store` as every API answer (T25: a cached copy would outlive a logout or a removed picture) with the picture's hash as its ETag; the lists put the picture's version in the URL (`?v=`), so a new picture is a new URL. (2) **Who may fetch:** the avatar its owner and the people who share a workspace with them (`Store::share_a_workspace`, checked on every request, so a removed member stops seeing it at once: where the members list shows their name), the banner its owner alone; everyone else, admins and the API token included, gets 404, the same as for no picture.
Server admins see accounts by username in Users, never a display name, so they get no more than that.
A browser fetch needs a link for that login and URL (`require_bytes`, P-1; `/api/users/{id}/avatar|banner` joined `auth::links::linkable`, the handler still checks who may see it). (3) **Text is text:** React renders the name, About you and instructions as text everywhere; no `ui:` block, no HTML. (4) **Framed:** About you is one paragraph (control characters and the line separators U+2028 and U+2029 refused on save, mapped again when sent) inside ‹ › in the settings line, its own ‹ › mapped to < > so it cannot close the quotation early, and it is left out of an untrusted (webhook) turn as Personalisation is; the instructions (control characters other than line breaks and tabs, and U+2028/U+2029, refused) follow a line of the harness's saying whose they are and that they change no setting, permission or tool, every line of theirs indented by two spaces with control characters and line separators replaced (`quoted_line`), then an end line; the whole block is quoted under the harness's lead as every section is (T43), so a forged `[harness]` or end line stays an indented, quoted line of theirs (`prefs::instructions_are_framed_line_by_line`, `profile_http::personalisation_reaches_only_the_owners_chats_framed`).
The reply choices are enums; their sentences are the server's (`STYLE_TEXT`, `TRAIT_TEXT`), never the client's. (5) **Whose:** loaded from the chat owner's settings in `Master::prompt` (whoever sends; a routine runs as its owner), not in an untrusted turn, not for an agent whose `personalisation` switch is off; a sub-agent (new or continued, T68) only when its own switch is on: the switch travels in its spec (`AgentSpec::no_personalisation`) and the node drops `personal` from what the child inherits of its parent turn (`node/src/session.rs` `inherited`, applied in `start_child` and `start_continue`, which build every child's turn: a foreground child, a continued one and a background one (X.24, T72), whose snapshot of the parent turn's standing sections is taken there; `node/tests/personal_subagents.rs`, foreground, continued and background); each built-in agent's definition sets it (X.31: Scout and Reviewer off, the rest on; `crates/agents/tests/agents.rs` `personalisation_is_on_unless_turned_off`, `node/tests/personal_subagents.rs` `the_builtin_scout_never_gets_personalisation_from_its_lead`, `master/tests/it/profile_http.rs` `builtin_agents_take_personalisation_as_their_definition_says`); another user's chat never reads them (tests above). (6) **Bounded and standing:** at most 1,024 characters of instructions and 280 of About you, so the section is under 16 KiB at its worst (every character four bytes and a line of its own: `personalisation_is_bounded_for_every_choice`), far under the 256 KiB section cap and the 4 MiB event limit; it is a standing section, sent again only when it changes (`agent-native/tests/context_log.rs` `personalisation_is_a_standing_section`), and passes the secret scrubbing of every section. (7) **Writes:** `PUT /api/me/settings` keeps the stored Personalisation and `PUT /api/me/settings/personalisation` changes only it, both under one lock (`Prefs::write_lock`); deleting an account deletes its settings row (the kv table had kept it before) and its pictures (cascade, files unlinked after).
**Residual:** About you and the instructions sent to a chat stay in its log (`context_sent`) until the chat is deleted, as memory facts do (T52); only the secrets the node knows are scrubbed, so a key pasted into the instructions is stored, sent and logged as a typed message would be; a 1 MiB banner on a slow uplink can hit the 30 s route timeout; within one tab type, two tabs saving the same part keep the last save; the API token has no profile.
**The e-mail for commits (T88)** is one more optional field of the same settings row (`Prefs::commit_email`, `PUT /api/me/settings`, Profile page): the person's own, read and written only through their own session (no admin, workspace admin or other member sees it in any list; the API token's settings refuse it); it never joins the model's settings line (`model_context` does not read it) and never a `context_sent` section.
Tests: `master/tests/it/profile_http.rs` `the_commit_email_is_checked_and_is_the_persons_own`.

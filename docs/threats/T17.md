# T17

## Threat

Files stored and served from the app origin (uploads, published builds) run script, phish, crash the viewer's tab, or outlive their deletion

## Mitigation

Kinds by magic bytes; inline only for sniffed raster image, audio and video (SVG and HEIC only in `<img>` context, with `Vary` and a variant ETag); everything else attachment + octet-stream; a per-response sandbox CSP that the guard keeps; nosniff, CORP same-origin; owner-only access (or shared-artifact members); corrected download extension; sniffed thumbnails; dimensions from headers and no browser decoding above 40 MP; no server-side decoding or unpacking; no import by URL.
Per-blob keys plus `secure_delete` and a WAL truncate remove deleted files and their keys from the live files (not from SSD or copy-on-write remnants of master.db): crypto-shredding, not encryption at rest, since the keys sit in master.db beside the files (audit L8; format and test vectors in docs/media.md); account deletion cleans files, FTS rows and the WAL at once.
Details: [docs/media.md](docs/media.md).

# T72

## Threat

**Background sub-agents** (ADOPTION_PLAN X.24, `docs/background-commands.md` §14): `delegate` with `background: true` returns at once and the child runs on after the parent's turn. (1) A prompt-injected lead starts children in a loop to spend money, or to outlive the per-turn caps. (2) A child that ends during the chat's deletion, a node restart, a `/compact`, a Retry or another callback; two at once; a lost or doubled delivery. (3) A huge result. (4) A writer child merged into a folder the parent or another child changed meanwhile, or merged mid-step. (5) The same child continued while it runs. (6) Depth: a child that starts one. (7) The master away when it ends. (8) A card nobody answers, or one answered because nobody watches. (9) Another account sees the child or its result. (10) An orphan process or folder.

## Mitigation

(1) Its own switch per agent (`DelegateCaps::background`, off but for the built-in Senior Teamlead), and the node decides: `[limits] background_agents_per_chat` (4) running at once, never raised by the master or the spec (`bound_delegation` clears the switch at 0 and below the top); each child counts as one of the turn's `max_subagents`, has the node's step cap, `background_agent_timeout_s` (30 min) of wall clock and a token cap: its share of the turn's sub-agent budget, reserved when it starts (an equal share of what is left over the turn's free sub-agent places; the caps a turn grants never add up to more than its budget; a start with a share of 0 is refused; `TurnExtra::max_tokens`, checked before each request); a callback turn cannot delegate, so the most per person's message is `max_subagents` children and then the `callback_turns` budget. (2) The child is an entry of the chat's background table, and its end takes the commands' one path (sent once, `unrecorded` counted, recorded by the session actor at a step boundary or between turns under the bg gate, `waiting_ends` and `CALLBACK_BUSY` as PR 116); a deleted chat records nothing and removes the folder unmerged; a node restart records `node_restarted` once at the start, with no callback. (3) `subagent::render`'s caps (24,000 characters of report, the merge's diff caps). (4) Merged when the end is recorded: at a step boundary (no parent tool runs) or between turns, with the foreground's three-way merge; a conflict is not applied, reported with its diff, the snapshot kept in the checkpoint store, the folder removed.
Two writers merge one after the other (one recorder per chat). (5) Refused while its entry runs or its end is not recorded (`undelivered_child`). (6) Refused in a sub-agent's turn, by the loop and by the node. (7) The end is in the node's log; it replays at the next hello and calls back only within 60 minutes (§4.4), else waits for the person. (8) The same card as a foreground child's, in its own chat, answered by the owner only, denied as cancelled at its wall clock; never auto-approved. (9) Its chat is a child of the owner's chat, shown through the same checks; the result is in the owner's chat only. (10) Every stop cancels its turn (in a `sandbox` chat its commands' guards end with it; a `host` child's foreground command has none: the `host` leftover below); a restart's start removes child folders.
**Residuals:** the token cap is checked before each request, so a child can pass its share by its last request (bounded by one request: its context and its answer); its citation labels do not resolve in the parent; a merge delivered between turns is in no turn's checkpoint, so Undo never reverts it (one delivered at a step boundary is that turn's, undone with it); so is a change it makes in the chat's folder through one of the chat's MCP servers (its own Connectors switch, the owner's decision of 2026-11-18, T47): between the chat's turns in no checkpoint, during a turn of the chat in that turn's; a crash between a writer's merge and the append of its end makes the restart's note say nothing was merged while the merge is in the folder (F2; the foreground path has the same window); a `host` chat has them like a `sandbox` chat (the owner, 2026-11-20: "dont, like if user selected host project workspace, why do we need to limit it? its his choice"; the child runs in its parent's mode, so its foreground command is a plain process group: a stop kills the group, a process it detached with `setsid` survives and a `kill -9` of the node can leave it, as for any host foreground command; tests `cli/tests/background_agents.rs` `*host*`); there is no node-wide count of background children and nothing about them in `doctor`; a sub-agent's own chat is deleted with its parent's (as for foreground children: `Master::delete_session` deletes every chat below it, deepest first, the chat last; one that cannot be deleted leaves the chat in place to delete again; a child started by a turn that was still running when the delete began is swept after, best effort, and its node is told again if it was away).

## Adds

Agent avatars as flat blobs (the owner, 2026-11-20: "rework agent avatars, they must have sort of those styling", with three reference pictures of flat blob characters with pill eyes and a grid picker over a colour row)

## Safeguards and abuse tests

**Built.**
Twenty-one soft blob shapes, one flat colour each, two pill eyes with a glint (white, or dark on the light colours), twelve colours, the picker a grid of shapes over round swatches; the 2.5D look and its tokens are gone.
Names of the six old shapes and the five eye styles stay valid; the built-in agents have hand-picked looks and every other agent's default is drawn from all shapes and colours (an unconfigured agent changes its look once).
Second round (the owner's references again): the eyes are about twice as large (the default tall pill is a third of the body's height), re-placed per shape and kept inside every body in every expression; `hat` is a pointed hat with a bent tip and a brim, `rocket` a rocket with a rounded nose, two fins and a flat base; the mushroom's face moved up onto its cap.
Accessories and a free colour are not built.
